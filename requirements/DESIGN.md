## Brand & Style

The design system embodies modern institutional prestige, accessibility, and forward-looking academic empowerment for Southeast Asia University (SAU). It bridges traditional academic authority with progressive digital self-service for incoming students, parents, and administrative staff.

The emotional tone balances institutional trust, clarity, and motivational momentum. Applying to university is a high-stakes life transition; the interface minimizes anxiety through clean structure, transparent progress feedback, unambiguous form architectures, and a modern civic dignity. 

Drawing from contemporary clean institutional aesthetics, the system leverages:
- Crisp architectural surfaces grounded by deep institutional navy and energetic royal blue.
- Academic optimism infused through warm amber highlights.
- Clear structural division with low visual noise, deliberate typographic hierarchy, and purposeful whitespace that accommodates bilingual Thai-Latin scripts seamlessly.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an authoritative yet welcoming academic environment. 

- **Primary (`#072044`)**: Deep academic navy, anchoring primary headers, high-importance navigation bars, authoritative badges, and active layout states.
- **Secondary (`#0072BA`)**: Dynamic university blue, powering interactive touchpoints, primary action buttons, active stepper nodes, and links.
- **Tertiary (`#ECBD1C`)**: Academic gold/amber, used intentionally for milestone accomplishments, scholarship banners, alert notices, and active review indicators.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Balanced slate gray managing supporting interface elements, secondary icons, and disabled states.

Backgrounds default to `surface-canvas` (`#F8FAFC`) with cards set against pure `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`). Form fields leverage `border-subtle` (`#E2E8F0`) with crisp `border-focus` (`#1A56DB`) rings on activation. Status colors guarantee instant visual feedback for ID verification, validation errors, and document upload verifications.

## Typography

The typography system is engineered specifically for clean bilingual Thai and Latin legibility. 

- **Display & Headings (Prompt)**: A geometric, contemporary sans-serif engineered for Thai signage and editorial interfaces. Its open loops and structural metrics provide visual authority and immediate clarity in headers, steppers, and structural titles.
- **Body & Longform (Sarabun)**: A refined humanist sans-serif offering superior readability for dense administrative disclosures, terms of service, form labels, and instructions. Increased line heights (`1.5` to `1.65`) accommodate Thai tone marks and vowel diacritics without clipping.
- **Labels & Microcopy (Prompt)**: Ensures crisp contrast in badges, stepper status nodes, button labels, and input labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The portal employs a structured 12-column responsive grid system centered within a maximum width container of `1200px` for portal views and `880px` for focused multi-step registration forms.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapters
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, `2rem` (`32px`) external margins. Complex form segments support multi-column layouts (e.g., 4-col personal title, 4-col first name, 4-col last name).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters, `1.5rem` (`24px`) margins. Two-column grouped inputs.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, `1rem` (`16px`) margins. Forms collapse strictly to a single column flow to maximize tapping targets and eliminate horizontal strain.

### Vertical Spacing & Rhythm
All spacing adheres strictly to an 8-point baseline rhythm. Form groups use `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) for macro separation, `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) between related field clusters, and `space-xs` (`0.25rem`) between input labels and controls.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined layered surface model paired with ambient shadows to maintain an airy, pristine academic ambiance without skeuomorphic heaviness.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Flat `#F8FAFC` slate background, receding beneath active work areas.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` surrounded by a soft 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Dropdowns)**: Menus, select popups, and date pickers: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 35, 66, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 35, 66, 0.04)` with a crisp `#E2E8F0` hairline boundary.
- **Level 3 (Modal & Trust Drawers)**: Verification modals and document preview viewports: `0 20px 35px -8px rgba(7, 32, 68, 0.16)`.
- **Focus Rings**: Active interactive components receive an offset focus state: `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 114, 186, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design system utilizes rounded geometric curvature (`roundedness: 2`) delivering approachable professionalism.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Applied to text input fields, dropdown triggers, standard buttons, and small preview thumbnails.
- **Medium Radius (`0.75rem` / `12px`)**: Applied to inner sub-cards, document upload drop zones, and alert containers.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / `16px`)**: Applied to primary registration card modules, modal dialogs, and verification summary cards.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Reserved for stepper progress badges, tag chips, counter badges, and category labels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#0072BA` background with white text, font `Prompt Medium`, `0.5rem` border radius, and `12px 24px` padding. Hover: `#005C96`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: White background with 1px border `#CBD5E1` and text `#072044`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`.
- **Accent / Final Submission**: Solid `#072044` with subtle gold accent border or highlight for irreversible admission submission steps.

### Multi-step Progress Indicator
- Horizontal track connecting numbered circular milestone indicators.
- **Completed**: `#16A34A` background with checkmark icon.
- **Active Step**: `#0072BA` ring with white center and pulsing badge outline; text bold `Prompt`.
- **Upcoming Step**: `#E2E8F0` background with `#94A3B8` numeric index.

### Input Fields & Selects
- 48px minimum touch height for universal touch/mouse accessibility.
- Permanent crisp labels set above the field using `label-md` (`Prompt`), including a red accent asterisk (`#DC2626`) for mandatory fields.
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#CBD5E1`. Active focus transitions to `#0072BA` border with 3px focus glow.
- Dropdowns for complex Thai title selects (`คำนำหน้าชื่อ`) feature scrollable searchable lists with grouped administrative categorizations.

### Document Upload Zones
- Dashed border (`2px dashed #94A3B8`) on `#F8FAFC` background.
- Clear icon indicator with file type restrictions (`PDF, JPG ไม่เกิน 5MB`).
- Inline state chips indicating `กำลังตรวจสอบ` (Checking), `สมบูรณ์` (Uploaded), or `ไม่ผ่าน` (Rejected).

### Cards & Group Containers
- White surface `#FFFFFF` bordered by `#E2E8F0` with subtle elevation.
- Section titles marked by a vertical 4px left-border accent in university navy (`#072044`).

### Trust Badges & System Confirmation
- Ministry of Higher Education (อว.) compliance badge and SSL cryptographic indicator pinned to form footers.
- Dual-key visual confirmation cards for National ID and phone verification states.
